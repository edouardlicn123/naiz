waitanima — Wait for Animation

Created: 2026-08-29

waitanima{} pauses scenario progression until the current animation has finished playing.

1. Syntax

waitanima{}
ParameterTypeDescription
——No parameters

2. Example

playanima(){opening}
waitanima{}

Input is swallowed while waiting (no line skipping, no animation abort); a once animation resumes the scenario by itself when it ends, with no keypress needed. Use it together with playanima — see sc15-playanima.html.
