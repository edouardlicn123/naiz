Transition Settings

Created: 2026-07-27

The Naiz engine can play a full-screen visual transition when scenes switch. You configure it in the project config.toml, and the build compiles it into the engine.

1. Scene transitions

A scene jump (the scene() command) plays a full-screen transition. There are 7 choices:

TypeKey nameDescription
Normal cut Switches immediately with no transition effect. The fastest option.
Fade pfade Palette fade-out: the whole 256-color table is interpolated towards black frame by frame, and background pixels are fully preserved.
Vertical blinds vblinds 20 vertical strips. The black edge advances from left to right frame by frame; each strip grows from narrow to full width until everything is black, and uncovered areas keep the original picture.
Horizontal blinds hblinds 20 horizontal strips. The black edge advances from top to bottom frame by frame; each strip grows from narrow to full width until everything is black.
Forward diagonal blinds dblinds 20 diagonal strips, direction ↖↘. The black edge advances along the diagonal frame by frame.
Reverse diagonal blinds rdblinds 20 diagonal strips, direction ↗↙. The black edge advances along the diagonal frame by frame.
Checker checker 16×10 squares of 40px. Odd-rank and even-rank cells black out in two waves along the diagonal; the wavefront keeps the checker texture the whole way, and the last frame is fully black.

2. config.toml settings

Configure it through the following fields in the project config.toml; the build compiles it into the engine automatically (no settings.txt needed):

SectionFieldDefaultDescription
[transition] type pfade Scene transition type. Options: cut pfade vblinds hblinds dblinds rdblinds checker
[transition] frames 16 Scene transition frame count (1–64). 16 frames ≈ 0.3 seconds at 60fps

Full example:

[transition]
type = "checker"
frames = 32

3. Blind direction diagrams

The blind family cuts the screen into 20 strips, and every strip grows from narrow to full black in sync (the black edge advances in one direction: everything before the edge is already black, everything after keeps the original picture). The examples use vblinds (left to right) and hblinds (top to bottom).

Vertical blinds (vblinds)

Strip direction is vertical; the black edge advances from left to right. 20 strips × 32px.

Step 0:  │░░░░░░░░│░░░░░░░░│░░░░░░░░│
Step 1:  │████░░░░│████░░░░│████░░░░│    ← each strip darkens from its left edge first, the boundary moves right
Step N:  │████████│████████│████████│    ← everything is black

░ = original picture visible, ▌ = target black

Horizontal blinds (hblinds)

Strip direction is horizontal; the black edge advances from top to bottom. 20 strips × 20px.

Step 0:  ──░░░░░░──  ──░░░░░░──
         ──░░░░░░──  ──░░░░░░──
Step 1:  ──████░░──  ──████░░──  ← each strip darkens from its top edge first, the boundary moves down
         ──░░░░░░──  ──░░░░░░──
Step N:  ──████████──  ──████████──  ← everything is black

Forward diagonal blinds (dblinds)

Diagonal strip direction ↖↘ (backslash direction). 20 strips × 32px; the black edge advances along the diagonal.

Dir:     ╲╱╲╱╲╱
         ╱╲╱╲╱╲
         ╲╱╲╱╲╱

The position coordinate (x + y) % 32 decides which strip each pixel belongs to, and the edge sweeps through in that coordinate order.

         ╲╱╲╱╲╱
         ╱╲╱╲╱╲

The position coordinate (x + h-1-y) % 32 decides which strip each pixel belongs to.

3.5 Checker

The screen is cut into 16×10 squares of 40px; the rank of cell (i,j) is r = i+j (0..24). Even-rank cells black out in sequence along the diagonal during the first half of the transition, and odd-rank cells fill in during the second half. Adjacent cells always differ in rank by 1 (opposite parity), so two adjacent cells never turn black in the same frame and the wavefront keeps the checker texture the whole way.

Activation timing (32 frames):

  rank r          activation frame            example cells
  even r=0,2,…,24  →  (31·r)/48          (0,0)→0, (0,2)→1, (0,4)→2 …
  odd  r=1,3,…,23  →  (31·(24+r))/48     (0,1)→16, (0,3)→17, (0,23)→30 …

All 160 cells activate once within 32 frames; the last odd rank (r=23) turns black on frame 30, so frames 30–31 are fully black (R=24 is even, and the black screen finishes 1 frame before the last one).

4. Notes

  • Diagonal blind performance: dblinds and rdblinds advance the diagonal strips row by row every frame, which costs a few more small rectangle fills than vertical/horizontal blinds, but drawing is still incremental per frame (only the newly covered narrow strips are drawn), so the overhead stays manageable.
  • Original picture preserved: the blind family keeps the original pixels until the edge sweeps over them; pfade preserves the whole background image. cut has no intermediate state.
  • Frame count range: transition.frames takes 1–64. 8–32 frames is recommended for scenes.
  • Frame count = 1: visually equivalent to cut mode (finishes in a single frame).
