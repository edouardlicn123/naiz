bg — Background Switch

Created: 2026-07-27

bg() switches the scene background, or closes the dialogue box and restores the background area.

1. Syntax

bg(effect[, transition]){key}
bg(hidedialog)

The object (the background key) goes in the curly-brace payload; the parenthesis slots are reserved for the effect / transition parameters (placeholders for now, not in effect yet). This follows the same convention as cg(){key}: "object in braces, parentheses = parameters ± keyword".

ParameterTypeDescription
keystringAsset name, matching asset_map.name (not filename), written in the curly-brace payload
effectstringDisplay mode (normal etc.), parenthesis parameter slot (currently a placeholder)
transitionstringOptional transition parameter (currently a placeholder)

1.1 Subcommands

CommandDescription
bg(hidedialog)Closes the dialogue box and restores the background area (keyword directive, no payload)

2. Example

bg(normal){splbg}
bg(normal, normal){logo}
bg(normal, normal){powered}
bg(hidedialog)

Note: the bg(key) form (key written inside the parentheses) is deprecated and is rejected by the engine and the scenario validator — the background key must go inside the braces.
