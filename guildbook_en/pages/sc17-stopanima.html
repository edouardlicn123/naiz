stopanima — Stop Animation

Created: 2026-08-29

stopanima{} stops the currently playing animation immediately and wakes the scenario.

1. Syntax

stopanima{}
ParameterTypeDescription
——No parameters

2. Example

playanima(loop){bg_anim}
...
stopanima{}

Use it to interrupt a loop animation, or to end a once animation early. It is usually paired with playanima / waitanima (see sc15-playanima.html, sc16-waitanima.html).
