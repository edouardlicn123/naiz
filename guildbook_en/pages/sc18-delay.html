delay — Scenario Delay

Created: 2026-08-29

delay(seconds) pauses scenario progression for the given number of seconds.

1. Syntax

delay(seconds)
ParameterTypeDescription
secondsnumberSeconds to pause; based on the 60Hz frame counter, 60 seconds maximum

2. Example

delay(2)

Implemented on the 60Hz frame counter; anything above 60 seconds is treated as 60 seconds. If you need to wait for an animation instead of a fixed duration, use waitanima{} (see sc16-waitanima.html).
