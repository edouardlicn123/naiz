cgvmenu / musicmenu — Gallery Mode

Created: 2026-07-27
Last updated: 2026-09-11

cgvmenu() opens the CG gallery; musicmenu() is the music gallery — still a reserved stub with no effect yet.

1. Syntax

cgvmenu()
musicmenu()

Neither takes parameters; calling them jumps to the CG gallery scene.

2. CG Gallery (cgvmenu, implemented)

  • Grid: full-screen black background + title, 4×2 thumbnail cells per page; "< / > / page number" for paging at the top, "Back" at the bottom right
  • Unlock bitmap: locked CGs show as grey placeholders; unlock state is read from the save (cached once on entry)
  • Preview: select an unlocked cell and press Enter / click to enter the full-screen preview; the preview is drawn on top of the gallery background (returning puts you back at the same grid position)
  • Rendering: the grid is drawn into a separate menu composite buffer (menu_layer, 0.2.093); focus moves use incremental partial publish, and the whole base is restored on exit
  • Collection progress: shown above the grid, updated in step with unlocks
