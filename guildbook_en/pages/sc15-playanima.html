playanima — Play Animation

Created: 2026-08-29

playanima{} plays a .ANI animation asset in the scenario. For how animations are made, see the Animation Design section.

1. Syntax

playanima{name}
playanima(once){name}
playanima(loop[,sec]){name}
ParameterTypeDescription
namestring.ANI asset name, matching asset_map.name
once / loopmodifierPlayback mode; when the modifier is omitted it defaults to once
secnumberTotal duration in seconds (overrides the container tick table); with loop it resets on expiry

2. Example

playanima(){opening}
playanima(loop,8){rain}

The sec duration rounds up to a tick budget (sec*60+0.999999); when sec is given the animation always ends on expiry (loop only wraps the frame sequence, it does not extend the duration). See devdocs/80.
